# Voluntary job-seeker walkthrough

Prepared November 2, 2026. This is a test script for the user to share after agreeing with a participant. No one has been contacted and no actual job-seeker validation has occurred.

## Setup and consent

Use a local or approved protected preview with a disposable account. Explain that the app is a prototype and ask permission to observe. Use a synthetic résumé/job or redact personal details; no need to supply employer-confidential information. Do not request payment details. Keep AI generation out of the initial walkthrough unless the participant and user explicitly choose to use the provider; seeded analysis examples are synthetic.

Offer a 15–20 minute session. Let the participant stop or skip a task. Ask separately before recording; written observations without identifying details are sufficient. On shared devices, leave recovery off. The browser-width check performed during development is not evidence of physical-phone usability.

## Tasks, without coaching

1. Save an opportunity from a pasted description. Explain what makes it worth applying to.
2. Attach a résumé snapshot, open the sample analysis, and save a revised cover letter. Find the earlier version again.
3. Record a submission with the original résumé and letter. Reopen exactly what was submitted after another letter edit.
4. Set the next useful action and date. Find it on Today, reschedule it, complete it, and reopen it from Completed actions.
5. On their own private browser, optionally enable device recovery, type a harmless note, close the workspace, reload, and restore for review. Ask whether the difference between a device draft and a database save is clear. Save explicitly, then sign out.

Do not submit a real application, send a recruiter message, trigger payment, or infer an interview appointment. The observer should note where the participant looks and hesitates before explaining controls.

## Record useful evidence

| Observation | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| First useful workspace | Time to save the job and identify a next step; assistance required |
| Material confidence | Can they find the exact submitted résumé/letter without coaching? |
| Recovery trust | Can they tell restored edits from confirmed database saves? |
| Today usefulness | Can they explain why an item appears and change its schedule? |
| Phone behavior | Device/browser, keyboard overlap, date picker, scrolling, and control reachability |
| Missing value | Repeated typing, lost context, unclear suggestions, and the next feature they would actually use |

Summarize observations and uncertainty. Do not claim interview/offer improvements from a match score or a completed walkthrough. Agree on any follow-up separately; no automated outreach or tracking is included. Choose the next feature from repeated observed problems: interview/calendar support, contacts, traceable résumé suggestions, or faster capture.
